Part 1

The Passive Voice

Three easy steps:

①Put the object first.
②Add is / are + past participle.
③Add by + who does it.
Sharks eat fish. ⬇ Fish are eaten by sharks.

Part 2

Passive: No and Questions

No = is / are + not + past participle
Butterflies aren't seen in cold seasons.
Question = Is / Are + ... + past participle?
Are dinosaur bones found in the mountains?
Yes, it is!
A tortoise is found in the mountains.
Remember: aren't = are + not  ·  isn't = is + not

Reading

Three Animal Homes 🏠

🌊 Water

Some animals live in the water. Fish, sharks, and octopuses live in the ocean. The water is salty and cold.

☀️ Desert

The desert is hot and dry. Camels, snakes, and tortoises live in the desert.

🌳 Forest

Forests have many trees. Birds build nests in the trees. Tigers live in the forest.

Writing

My Animal ✏️

Write about your favorite animal. Use this frame:

This animal lives in a ___ environment.
The ___ is where ___ live.
___ are found in ___.

Model answer:

My favorite animal is the dolphin. It lives in the sea.
The sea is where dolphins live.
Dolphins are found in the ocean. 🐬
Useful expressions: This animal lives in a / an ... environment. · ... is where ... is / are found.
